# User manual

Find guidance for account, product, and administration tasks. Browse the navigation or search for a page or an action you want to complete.

## Get started

1. If you do not have an account, start with [Sign in and register]({{DOCS_BASE_URL}}/en/login-register).
2. After signing in, choose a console from the site switcher in the application. Available entries depend on your permissions.
3. Choose a topic from the documentation navigation or search for an action. Switching languages keeps you on the current article.
4. To share guidance, use the copy page link action.

If you forgot your password, see [Reset your password]({{DOCS_BASE_URL}}/en/forgot-password).

## Choose a console

Products and management functions use separate sites. The site switcher groups user sites, product consoles, user consoles, and operations, and shows only entries you are permitted to access. The public home page also offers a site overview.

After you sign in, each site's overview shows relevant statistics and common actions according to the site and your permissions. Cross-site actions open the appropriate site. Known legacy links and pages opened on the wrong site redirect to the correct location while retaining filters and anchors.

## Find help

Search covers titles, tags, and article text. Try a feature name or terms such as “password”, “balance”, or “permission”. Search matches the complete text entered; if there are no results, try a shorter phrase or a different term. Available features, quotas, and management actions may depend on your permissions and the current service configuration.

In the application, hover over your avatar to view account information and balance. On desktop, the shortcut bar on the right provides common actions according to your permissions. You can also continue to the [application]({{APP_BASE_URL}}) or [API reference]({{SWAGGER_DOCS_URL}}).
